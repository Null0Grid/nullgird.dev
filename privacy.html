PRIVACY

The short version: no accounts with us, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing you write, sync or browse is sent to us. What the apps keep, they keep on your own devices or on a server you run.

This website (nullgrid.dev)

nullgrid.dev is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and there's no sign-up or form. GitHub, as the host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Null Search (search.nullgrid.dev)

  • Web searches: your search terms are sent to the Null Search server so it can fetch results. They're used only to answer that search. We don't build profiles, sell searches or use them for advertising.
  • Your notes and files stay off our server. When Null Search shows results from Null Notes or Null Drive, your browser asks them directly: Null Notes on your own computer, Null Drive at the address you entered. Those queries, results and tokens never reach the Null Search server.
  • Settings you enter, such as a Drive address and search token, are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Clear them any time from the settings panel.
  • search.nullgrid.dev is reached through Cloudflare, which handles the network traffic under its own privacy policy.

Null Grid (the browser)

Null Grid has no accounts, analytics or tracking. What it keeps stays on your PC:

  • History: only the names of sites you visit (like "example.com"), never full addresses, pages or searches. At most 100 sites, deleted after 7 days.
  • Saved logins and your Null Drive token are encrypted by Windows for your user account.
  • Cookies and site data are kept in memory and erased when you close the browser. Burner tabs erase theirs sooner.
  • Favorites, loadouts, settings and pages you save to the Vault stay on your PC.

It connects to other services only when a feature needs it: websites you visit, the search engine you pick, your own Null Drive if you connect one, Ghostery's servers for public ad-block lists, Google Translate when you press translate, radio stations when you play one, and the AI service you choose for the side panel. The full list is in Null Grid's own privacy policy.

Null Notes

  • Your vault is a folder of files on your computer. No account and no cloud. Nothing leaves your computer unless you turn on sync.
  • Locked notes are encrypted with your passphrase. Their text is never written to disk unencrypted.
  • Sync, if you turn it on, sends your notes only to the Null Drive server you enter. With an end-to-end encrypted Drive account, notes are encrypted on your computer first.
  • Search: a small service on your own computer (127.0.0.1) lets Null Search find your notes. It answers only Null Search, and only with your pairing token.

Null Drive

  • Null Drive is software you run on your own server. Your files, account and devices live there, not with us. We never receive them.
  • Whoever runs the server can see the files on it, unless the account uses end-to-end encryption. Then file contents and names are encrypted on your devices, and the server only stores ciphertext.
  • The desktop app talks only to the server you connect it to. A Null Search token can search and nothing else.

Null Scribe

Null Scribe has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, ads or crash reporting, and it doesn't connect to any server we run. We never receive your recordings, transcripts or summaries.

  • Everything runs on your computer. Transcription (whisper.cpp) and summaries (the built-in llama.cpp model) run locally. The summary engine listens only on 127.0.0.1, so it can't be reached from your network.
  • Microphone and app audio are used only while you're recording. In Meeting mode it captures only the apps you pick, like Zoom or Discord. It never records in the background.
  • Stored on your device: recordings, transcripts, summaries, settings and the models, in the app's data folder. Your Null Drive token is encrypted with your system's secure storage.
  • When data leaves your computer: only if you choose it.
    • On first launch, the models download from Hugging Face, which sees your IP address. No recordings are sent.
    • With Ollama as the engine, transcripts go to the Ollama address you enter, usually your own server.
    • Send to Drive uploads the files you choose to your Null Drive server.
    • Send to Notes writes a note into your vault folder. Nothing goes over the network.
    • Open in Claude copies the summary to your clipboard and opens claude.ai. Nothing is sent unless you paste it, and then Anthropic's privacy policy applies.
  • Deleting your data: delete any session in the app (Session → Delete). Uninstalling the Store version removes its data folder. On Linux, delete ~/.config/Null Scribe.
  • Get consent before you record. Recording people without their agreement is illegal in many places, and telling them is your responsibility.

The full version is in Null Scribe's own privacy policy.

Downloads and app stores

Downloads come from GitHub releases and container images, and from the Microsoft Store. Each handles your download under its own privacy policy. We don't receive personal information from them.

Children
