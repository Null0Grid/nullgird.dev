> desktop web browser v5.1.0

NULL//GRID

The browser of the Null Grid family, our take on Chrome.

Vertical tabs, burner tabs that wipe themselves, and a history that only keeps site names. Searches go through Null Search, and your own pages and files can show up right beside the web.

Microsoft StoreLinux .deb · .rpm

What's inside

Everything can be switched off in one Settings page.

Burner tabs

A burner tab gets its own throwaway storage that wipes itself. Nothing it touches is kept.

Short memory

History keeps only site names, never full pages or searches: the last 100 sites, for 7 days.

Ad and tracker blocking

Built in, and pausable per site when a page needs it.

Login vault

Saved logins are encrypted with your system's secure storage.

Loadouts

Save a whole set of open tabs by name, then deploy it or swap it back in later.

Vault Matches

The start page searches through Null Search. Switch on Vault and your saved pages, Null Notes and Null Drive files merge right into the web results.

Your Null Drive

Add your Drive's address and token in Settings. The browser connects to it directly, on the same PC or a server on your network, never through the search server.

AI side panel

Claude by default, or ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio.

Extras

A radio player in the sidebar, dark pages on any site, reader mode, save pages as Markdown, picture-in-picture, QR code to your phone, a password generator, translation, tabs that sleep, and the NODE_GRID SIM mini-game.
